Arduino speed loop

Example Uno wiring uses encoder A on D2, encoder B on D4, direction on D7 and PWM on D9. The encoder scale is configured to 700 rising edges of A per output-shaft revolution for the 100:1 12GA-N20 gearmotor. With 34 mm wheels this is approximately 6.55 counts/mm. Verify the direction polarity. Speed is in RPM and command is in percent.
